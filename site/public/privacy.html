Privacy

Last updated 29 October 2026

This page covers the TapWheel app and this website. TapWheel is made by Jax (muxover). If anything here is unclear, write to contact@muxover.com.

The app

TapWheel doesn't collect, send or store anything about you. It makes no network connections: no account, no telemetry, no crash reports, no ads and no update checks. The Microsoft Store version is updated by the Store itself.

Files you drop on the wheel are processed on your PC by engines that ship inside the app, such as FFmpeg and the background removal model. The results are saved next to the original, or in the subfolder or folder you choose in Settings. Nothing is uploaded.

To notice when you tap Shift, Ctrl or Alt during a drag, or tap one twice on files selected in File Explorer, TapWheel watches keyboard and mouse input while it runs. It only reacts to those keys, Tab and Esc, and keeps no record of what you type or click. Every click and every other key goes on to Windows unchanged; a tap TapWheel uses during a drag from File Explorer stays with TapWheel, so Explorer doesn't read it as a link or a move.

TapWheel saves its settings (your keys, the options in its window, how you arranged the wheels and the ring choices you kept, how often you pick each slice so favourites can move to the top, and the folders you last moved files to) in %APPDATA%\TapWheel\settings.toml on your PC. Uninstalling the Store version removes it, and Remove from this PC in the zip version's tray menu deletes it.

When something goes wrong, TapWheel writes the error to %LOCALAPPDATA%\TapWheel\tapwheel.log on your PC so the problem can be found later. It holds error messages, the names of the files involved and a short note about the PC it runs on (processor cores, memory, graphics card and its video encoder, drive type, power and screen rate, so a report can be matched to the hardware) and how much memory TapWheel itself holds while it waits, never what you type, keeps itself under 256 KB and is deleted with the rest by Remove from this PC. It never leaves your PC unless you send it to someone. Report a problem, in TapWheel’s settings, opens a new GitHub issue in your browser filled in with TapWheel’s version, your Windows version, your screens and the end of this log, with your user name taken out. You read it first, and nothing is sent unless you post it.

In the Microsoft Store version, Windows may keep these two files in TapWheel's own folder under %LOCALAPPDATA%\Packages instead. TapWheel still finds them at the paths above, and uninstalling removes them.

Open sample files, in TapWheel's window, puts four small files to practise on in Documents\TapWheel samples. Remove from this PC deletes those four files, and the folder if nothing else is in it.

This website

The site sets no cookies and has no ads or trackers. It uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views and load times without cookies and without following you across other sites. The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes standard request data such as IP addresses to serve pages and block attacks.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. TapWheel's source code is public on GitHub, so anyone can check what the app does.

# TapWheel v0.2.0 — Reach and control

**Release date:** RELEASE-DATE

### Install

```text
Get TapWheel from the Microsoft Store, or download TapWheel-0.2.0-windows-x64.zip (or -arm64.zip for Arm PCs) from this release, unzip it and run tapwheel.exe
```

---

## Summary

The wheel no longer needs a drag, and it can be arranged the way you work. Select files and tap a wheel's key twice, or right-click them, and the wheel opens beside them. Each wheel can be pinned, trimmed and reordered for each kind of file. TapWheel also reads the PC it runs on: video compresses on the graphics card when it can, long jobs show the time left, and heavy work waits while Battery saver is on. There's a native build for Arm PCs, and screen readers hear the wheel.

---

## What's included

### Without dragging

- Select files in File Explorer or on the desktop and tap a wheel's key twice. The wheel opens beside the selection, ready for the keyboard. Settings can switch this to Ctrl+Shift+Space or turn it off.
- The Store version adds TapWheel to Explorer's right-click menu, at the top of the menu on Windows 11.

### Your own wheels

- A Wheels page in Settings: pin, hide and move slices, for each wheel and each kind of file, and pick the wheel's size and colour.
- Keep a tool's settings as a choice on its ring from the tool's window, like a smaller video for chats.
- Results can go next to the original, into a subfolder, or into one folder you pick. A file that's already there is never replaced.
- Batch rename has Undo.

### Fits your PC

- TapWheel reads the processor, memory, graphics card, drive, power and screen when it starts, and Settings, General shows what it read.
- How many batches run at once follows the PC. Video and background removal wait while Battery saver is on.
- Compress on Balanced uses the graphics card's video encoder (NVIDIA, Intel or AMD) when there is one, and the card names it. Settings can turn it off.
- Cards show the time left on long jobs, and say when a job will be slow on this PC.
- The wheel fits every screen, scaling and monitor layout.
- A native build for Windows on Arm, with its own FFmpeg and ONNX Runtime.

### Screen readers and the keyboard

- Screen readers hear the wheel, its slices and ring choices, page turns and finished jobs.
- On a wheel opened from the keyboard, Down reaches the ring choices.
- Choice groups in the tool windows and Settings move with the arrow keys and read as radio buttons.
- The newest job's buttons are in the tray menu.

### Looks

- The tray and window icon follow your accent colour and the taskbar's light or dark mode.
- Settings has more room, and choices take only the space they need.

### Help

- Report a problem in Settings, About opens a GitHub issue in your browser, filled in with the version, Windows, your screens and the end of the log, with your user name taken out. Nothing is sent until you post it.

### Removed

- Terminal is no longer on the Actions wheel.

### Website

- A new [tapwheel.app](https://tapwheel.app): the wheel to play with on the first screen, the new features, and a clearer privacy section.

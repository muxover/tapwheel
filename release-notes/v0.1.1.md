# TapWheel v0.1.1 — Steadier on big files

**Release date:** unreleased

### Install

```text
Get TapWheel from the Microsoft Store, or download TapWheel-0.1.1-windows-x64.zip from this release, unzip it and run tapwheel.exe
```

---

## Summary

A bug-fix release. It stops the crashes and wrong output found in a full review of v0.1.0, keeps the wheel quick while a video editor works on a large file, and opens the video editors without decoding the whole video first. There are no new features.

---

## What's included

### Big files and the wheel

- Compress on a 2.7 GB video no longer runs out of memory and closes TapWheel. The editors now read a video from disk in 4 MB pieces.
- Tapping Alt while a drag is going opens the wheel again with a video editor open, instead of Explorer showing "Create link". The keyboard and mouse hooks run on their own thread.
- Video editors open as soon as the file is read. Trim builds its filmstrip from 14 frames (1.3 s instead of up to 9.8 s on a 2.7 GB file), and Compress, Crop and Snapshot don't build one.
- Make GIF and Reverse no longer hold every frame of a video in memory. Reverse turns a long video around a piece at a time.
- The wheel fits on a small screen, such as 1366 by 768 at 125%.
- After 30 quiet seconds TapWheel gives memory back: the background-removal model (about 400 MB, loaded as soon as a picture's Tools wheel opened) and what a finished job or closed window left behind.
- Dropping thousands of files no longer holds up the wheel while TapWheel names the results.

### Crashes

- RTF, subtitle and INI files that made a conversion crash now convert.
- A damaged PSD or camera raw file fails on its card instead of closing the app.
- Very deep XML, HTML, YAML or TOML, and files that claim a huge size, are refused with a message.
- When the app does close on its own, the log says why, and a crash file is left in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\TapWheel\crash`.
- FFmpeg ends when TapWheel ends.

### Right output

- Edited photos no longer keep the old thumbnail. Redact drops the location, and Remove location also clears it from XMP.
- Two jobs at once never share their temporary files.
- Multi-part `.gz`, `.bz2` and `.xz` files extract in full.
- JSON keeps its key order and numbers, and CSV keeps its columns.
- Animated GIFs stay animated through the picture tools.
- Text keeps its encoding and BOM, and RTF reads its code page.
- Indent, TOML minify, renaming `.tar.gz` files and stripping details from PNG and WebP portraits all give the right result. Minify is gone for CSV.
- Extract unpacks hard links as copies, skips symbolic links and renames what Windows can't hold, and says so on the card.
- Batch rename never replaces a file that appeared after the preview.

### Tool windows

- Compress and Crop treated every video as a picture, and the tag editor treated sound files as photos. They know what they opened again.
- Open sample files works on every PC. The Microsoft Store version made the folder where Explorer couldn't see it; the files now go to `Documents\TapWheel samples`.
- Typing a width or height in Crop no longer jumps to another number while you type.
- The video quality words in Compress match the slider.
- Enter on Cancel cancels.
- Save comes back when a save is turned down, so it can be tried again.

### Website

- The site moved to [tapwheel.app](https://tapwheel.app). Old links keep working.
